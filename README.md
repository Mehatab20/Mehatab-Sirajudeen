# 🚀 Projects & Engineering Work

I believe projects are one of the best ways to learn engineering. These are some of the systems I've designed and worked on.

## 01. 🏥 SurgiMind — AI Surgical Decision Support Assistant

**An AI-powered clinical decision-support prototype designed to assist doctors through evidence-grounded insights and risk stratification.**

SurgiMind explores how Machine Learning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and local LLMs can be combined to build a more transparent and privacy-conscious clinical assistance system.

### Key Engineering Contributions

- **Machine Learning Pipeline:** Developed a Random Forest classification pipeline using MIMIC-III clinical datasets, achieving 80.77% accuracy in surgical risk classification.
- **Risk Stratification:** Implemented HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW risk classifications with a numerical risk score.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation:** Integrated FAISS-based retrieval using 171 surgical guidelines to ground generated responses in relevant medical information.
- **Privacy-Focused AI:** Integrated local LLM processing through Ollama to reduce dependence on external AI services.
- **Deterministic Safety:** Designed a three-tier reasoning architecture with a Clinical Rules Engine as a fallback when generative AI is unavailable.
- **Explainability:** Built a dashboard to present risk information and supporting clinical context.

### Technology Stack

`Python` `Scikit-learn` `Random Forest` `FAISS` `RAG` `Ollama` `Streamlit` `Flask`

**Engineering Focus:** Machine Learning, AI Safety, Explainability, Privacy, Fault Tolerance.

## 02. ♿ EchoTouch — Adaptive Multimodal Accessibility Framework

**Digital payments that adapt to every ability, without relying on sight, sound, or assistance.**

EchoTouch is an accessibility-focused financial technology concept developed to explore more independent digital payment experiences for blind, deaf-blind, elderly, and other users with accessibility needs.

### Key Engineering Contributions

- **Adaptive Interface:** Designed a touch-first interface with gesture-driven interactions and context-aware haptic feedback.
- **Six-Dot Braille Authentication:** Developed a virtual Braille PIN interaction concept based on a six-dot tactile arrangement.
- **Spatial Braille Replay Engine:** Designed frequency-encoded vibration patterns to communicate authentication feedback through touch.
- **Silent Authentication:** Explored a payment authentication experience that does not depend on spoken PINs or audible feedback.
- **Transaction Reliability:** Designed database safeguards using SERIALIZABLE isolation and state-machine locking to prevent race conditions and duplicate transactions.
- **AI Assistance:** Explored Whisper and Llama-based speech processing for accessible interactions and verbal toxicity detection.

### Technology Stack

`React Native` `Spring Boot` `PostgreSQL` `Whisper` `Llama 3.1` `Haptic Feedback`

**Engineering Focus:** Accessibility Engineering, FinTech, Human-Computer Interaction, Transaction Security.
